🦃 Challenge Rules

Welcome to our English Open House Booth! Each visitor can pick any of the 4 games:

🍽️ 1. Build Your Feast

  • Serve 5 traditional Thanksgiving foods on your plate.
  • Avoid fast food intruders (pizza, tacos, burgers).
  • Margin: Max 1 mistake (2 lives). 2 minutes time limit.

🥧 2. Trivia Rush

  • Answer 7 random multiple-choice questions in English.
  • Margin: Score 6 out of 7 correct to win. 2 minutes time limit.

🍂 3. Memory Match

  • Find all 8 vocabulary pairs on the 16-card board.
  • Margin: Unlimited flips! Just beat the 2-minute clock.

🎡 4. Category Wheel

  • Spin the wheel to discover your theme (*History, Food, Traditions, Vocabulary*).
  • Answer 3 multiple-choice questions from that category.
  • Margin: Score 2 out of 3 correct to win. 2 minutes time limit.
