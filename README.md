# 🦃 Thanksgiving Challenge - English Open House 🍂

An interactive, responsive, web-based Thanksgiving celebration game hub designed for an **English Project Open House**. 

Visitors can choose any of 4 festive mini-games, test their English vocabulary and cultural knowledge in under **2 minutes**, and win a **Golden Snack Ticket**!

---

## 🎮 The 4 Games & Rules

1. 🍽️ **Build Your Feast**
   - **Goal:** Serve 5 traditional Thanksgiving foods (*Roast Turkey, Cranberry Sauce, Mashed Potatoes, Pumpkin Pie, etc.*) onto the holiday plate.
   - **Rules:** Avoid fast-food distractors (pizza, tacos, burgers). Max 1 mistake allowed (2 hearts/lives). 2-minute timer.

2. 🥧 **Trivia Rush**
   - **Goal:** Answer 7 randomized multiple-choice questions in English (A2-B1 level).
   - **Rules:** Questions and option order shuffle every round from a 35+ question bank. Score at least 6 out of 7 to win (max 1 mistake). 2-minute timer.

3. 🍂 **Memory Match**
   - **Goal:** Find all 8 vocabulary pairs on a 16-card board (4x4 grid).
   - **Rules:** Pure time-trial! Unlimited flips with no penalty for mismatches. Beat the 2-minute clock to win.

4. 🎡 **The Category Wheel**
   - **Goal:** Spin the animated wheel to receive a random category (*History, Food & Feast, Traditions, Vocabulary & Riddles*).
   - **Rules:** Answer 3 thematic multiple-choice questions from that category. Score at least 2 out of 3 to win (max 1 mistake). 2-minute timer.

---

## 🌟 Key Features
- **100% Responsive:** Adapts seamlessly to desktop monitors, laptops, iPads/tablets, and mobile phones.
- **Native Web Audio API:** Synthesized chimes, buzzer, tick-tock countdown, wheel spinning, and fanfare without needing external audio files. Includes sound mute toggle.
- **Golden Snack Ticket & Confetti:** Displays an official digital voucher with a timestamp and unique pass code to claim a treat at the booth.
- **Fully Offline & Standalone:** Zero external runtime dependencies. Runs directly in any browser.
